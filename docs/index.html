A GAME ABOUT DECISIONS

The Door Study

Find your way. Meet your guide. Make your choice.

Which door will you choose?

Move the yellow player through the maze to the blue exit. Stay ahead of the red pursuer. Then choose a left or right door while a guide offers advice. The guide may be right or wrong.

Use arrow keys or the on-screen buttons. Each correct door earns 10 points. A wrong door costs health. There are three hallways, each with nine choices. The hallway clock starts after the maze.

Reach the blue exit. Keep moving!
