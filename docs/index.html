YOUR GOAL: STAY ONE STEP AHEAD

The way out is a decision away.

You are the yellow light in a winding maze. Behind you, a red dot gives chase. Reach the blue exit before it catches you. Beyond the maze, doors await and the clock begins.

Move quickly, choose carefully, and make it through all three hallways while preserving your health. A guide will offer help along the way. Whether you listen is up to you.

Find the exit. Use the arrow keys or on-screen arrows. If the red dot catches you, your run ends.

Choose your path. Each hallway has nine decisions. Use ← / → or the door buttons. A correct door earns 10 points; a wrong door costs 5 health.

Beat the clock. You begin with 100 health. Hallway limits are 90, 60, then 40 seconds, starting after each maze. Running out of time or health ends the run.
